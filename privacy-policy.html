1. Scope of this Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application "History of the World" (the "App") handles information relating to its users. It applies to the App distributed through Google Play, Amazon Appstore and HUAWEI AppGallery. By installing and using the App you confirm that you have read this Policy.

The App is an offline educational reference on world history with a quiz. It has no user accounts and no server backend operated by the developer. The versions differ only in advertising:

2. Personal data the developer collects

The developer does not collect, receive or store on any server any personal data about you. The App does not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number or any other identity data; it does not access your location (GPS), contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera; and the developer has not added any analytics or crash-reporting software (the advertising SDK's own data collection is described in sections 5 and 6).

In the Google Play version, the App asks for your year of birth at first launch only to choose suitable ads (section 5). The year itself is not saved; only the resulting age group is kept on your device and is never sent to the developer.

In the versions with advertising, the advertising partner described in sections 5 and 6 receives certain technical data directly from your device in order to deliver ads. The developer does not receive this data.

4. How we use information

The developer does not use personal data for any purpose. The locally stored data described in section 3 is used only to provide the App's functions. It is not used for profiling, marketing or automated decision-making by the developer.

5. Advertising in the Google Play version (1.8.0 and later)

Ads are shown in these situations: chapters 1–3 are free, and each further chapter opens after you watch one short ad to the end (after that it stays open for good, also without an internet connection); in the quiz you can choose to watch an ad for a second chance after a wrong answer, for a hint when you have no coins, or to double the coins for a round; and occasionally an ad is shown between quiz rounds. Ads are never shown when the App starts.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK, Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited), which participates in Google Play's Families Self-Certified Ads SDK Program. Because the App may be used by children, at first launch it asks for your year of birth (age-neutral question). No ad is requested before this question is answered. Depending on the answer:

Age groupHow ads are requested
Under 13Marked as directed to children (COPPA, "tag for child-directed treatment"): no personalized ads and no remarketing, the Android advertising ID is not sent, ad content limited to the "G" (general audiences) rating.
13–15Non-personalized ads only, in every country; in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the request is also marked as coming from a user under the age of consent; ad content up to the "T" (teen) rating.
16 and overIn the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown before any ad, and ads are personalized only if you agree; you can change your choice at any time with "Ad privacy settings" in the App menu (shown where required). Elsewhere, Google may show personalized ads; you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your Android settings. Ad content up to the "T" rating.

Ads can be closed and are clearly separated from the App's content.

To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects the following data directly from your device, according to Google's disclosure for developers: IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived); product interactions (for example app launches, taps and video views); diagnostic information (for example app performance and crash data); and device identifiers (the Android advertising ID — not for users under 13 — and the app set ID). Google uses this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention; it is encrypted in transit. The processing is carried out by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

6. Advertising and purchase in the Amazon Appstore version (1.8.0 and later)

The Amazon version shows ads from Liftoff Monetize (the Vungle SDK, operated by Vungle, Inc. / Liftoff Mobile, Inc.) after some quiz sessions. Before ads are shown, the App asks for your consent to personalized advertising; if you decline, only non-personalized ads are shown.

To deliver an ad, measure that it was shown and prevent fraud, the Vungle SDK processes technical data such as: IP address (from which an approximate location such as country or city can be derived), device model and manufacturer, operating system version, language and time-zone settings, screen size, network type, and events related to the ad (loaded, shown, closed, clicked). According to Liftoff, this data is encrypted in transit. The processing is carried out by Liftoff under its own privacy policy: Liftoff Privacy Policy and Ad Information.

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed entirely by the Amazon Appstore. The developer does not receive your payment details; the App only receives confirmation from Amazon that the purchase was made, so that ads can be turned off on your device. Amazon's handling of your data is governed by Amazon's privacy notice.

7. Sharing and transfer of data

  • The developer does not sell, rent or trade user data and holds no user data that could be shared or disclosed.
  • In the versions with advertising, the technical data listed in sections 5 and 6 is sent directly from your device to the advertising partner — Google (Google Play version) or Liftoff Monetize (Amazon version) — which may process it outside your country, including in the United States.
  • In the HUAWEI AppGallery version and in Google Play versions before 1.8.0 no data leaves your device.

9. Data security

The developer holds no personal data on servers. Data stored on your device is protected by the Android application sandbox. Data processed by the advertising partner is transmitted in encrypted form.

10. Children's privacy

The App is an educational product suitable for children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The App has no chat, no user-generated content and no account. In the Google Play version, users who state that they are under 13 receive only child-directed, non-personalized ads without the advertising ID, and users under 16 receive only non-personalized ads, as described in section 5. Parents or guardians who have questions, or who wish to request deletion of data processed by the advertising partner, can contact the developer at the e-mail address below or contact the advertising partner (Google or Liftoff) directly through its privacy policy.

11. Your rights

Under data-protection laws including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict and object to the processing of your personal data, the right to data portability and the right to withdraw consent. Because the developer holds no personal data about you, requests concerning data processed for advertising are best addressed to the advertising partner — Google (section 5) or Liftoff (section 6); the developer will help you with any request — write to info@artgroupproject.com, and we will reply within 30 days. You can always clear the data stored on your device as described in section 3.

Data stored on your device (section 3) is necessary to provide the functions you use. Non-personalized ads, their measurement and fraud prevention are based on the legitimate interest in funding the free App. Personalized ads in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are shown only with your consent: in the Google Play version you can change it with "Ad privacy settings" in the App menu; in the Amazon version you can withdraw it by clearing the App's data.
