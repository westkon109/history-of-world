1. Scope of this Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application "History of the World" (the "App") handles information relating to its users. It applies to the App distributed through HUAWEI AppGallery and Google Play, and to all of its functions. By installing and using the App you confirm that you have read this Policy.

The App is an offline educational reference on world history. It has no user accounts, no server backend operated by the developer, and no advertising or analytics components.

2. Personal data we collect

The developer does not collect, receive, store on any server, or transmit any personal data. Specifically, the App does not collect:

  • identity data — name, e-mail address, telephone number, date of birth, gender;
  • device identifiers — IMEI, MAC address, Android ID, advertising identifier (OAID/GAID), serial number;
  • location data — GPS coordinates, network-based location, Wi-Fi or cell-tower information;
  • contacts, call logs, SMS messages, calendar entries;
  • photographs, audio, video, or files stored on the device;
  • account credentials or payment information;
  • usage analytics, crash reports, behavioural or advertising profiles.

No registration is required to use the App, and no data is requested from you at any point.

4. How we use information

Because no personal data is collected, the developer does not use personal data for any purpose. The locally stored technical data described in section 3 is used solely to provide the App's core functionality: displaying historical content offline in your chosen language and restoring your reading position. It is not used for profiling, advertising, marketing, scoring, or automated decision-making.

5. Sharing, transfer and disclosure of data

No data is shared with anyone. In particular:

  • the developer does not sell, rent, or trade user data;
  • no data is transferred to third-party companies, partners, advertisers, or data brokers;
  • no data is transferred across borders, because no data is transmitted off the device at all;
  • no data is disclosed to public authorities, as the developer holds no user data that could be disclosed;
  • in the event of a change of App ownership, no user data would be transferred, because none is held.

6. Third-party SDKs and services

The App contains no third-party software development kits. There is no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK (such as Google Analytics, Firebase, HUAWEI Analytics), no crash-reporting SDK, no social-network SDK, and no payment SDK. The App does not use HUAWEI Mobile Services or Google Mobile Services APIs to obtain user data.

All content is packaged inside the installation file. The App does not require an internet connection to function and does not contact any server operated by the developer. Should you deliberately follow an external hyperlink from the App, the website you open will be governed by its own privacy policy, over which the developer has no control.
